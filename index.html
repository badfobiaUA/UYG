    Stand tutorial

    Start here if you are new: how Stands work, how to get one, and how every Stand in the game is obtained.

    1. Get a Stand

    Use a Stand Arrow to roll a random Arrow Stand, or a Rib Cage of the Saint's Corpse with Worthiness for a Rib Cage Stand. Until you own a Stand, other players' Stands are invisible to you.

    2. Summon and learn moves

    Summon your Stand with its summon key, then open your moveset in game and test every move on a training dummy or in a quiet server.

    3. Level up

    Stands and your character gain levels from quests, NPC fights and PvP. Higher levels unlock stronger options, so keep progressing.

    4. Upgrade

    Many Stands evolve with a Requiem Arrow or a Saint's Corpse part plus Worthiness. Some Stands have Skin variants, which you can roll through the pity system, a Lucky Arrow, or pure luck.

    All Stands

    Percentages are roll chances from the YBA Wiki. Worthiness is now a single skill tree node that costs 0 skill points. Planned Stands, not yet in the game: Diver Down, Catch The Rainbow and Anubis Silver Chariot. Check the wiki for changes after updates.

    PvP guides

    Habits that win fights no matter which Stand you roll.

    Fundamentals

    Block on purpose. Block when the other player commits to a big attack, then punish the gap after it ends. Holding block all fight loses to grabs and guard breaks.

    Control spacing. Stay just outside their reach, step in to hit, and step out. Your Stand's best range decides where you want the fight to happen.

    Track cooldowns. After an opponent uses a key move, you have a short window. Attack then.

    Combos and pressure

    Start with light hits to connect, then finish with your Stand's strongest move that follows without a gap. Practise one reliable combo until it is automatic, then add a second.

    Do not spam one move. Mix hits, blocks and movement so the opponent cannot predict you.

    Fighting common situations

    Against a faster player: block more, make them come to you and punish whiffs.

    Against a ranged player: close the distance with dashes and angles instead of walking straight at them.

    Against a group: keep your back to a wall, fight one player at a time and retreat to heal when you can.

    Mistakes to fix first

    Using every move the moment it is ready. Chasing a player who is running away. Fighting at low health instead of resetting. Switching Stands every day, so you never learn one fully.

    How to practise

    Record a fight, watch it back, and note the first moment you lost control. Fix that one habit, then record again.
